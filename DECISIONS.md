- 2026-11-09: SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-05 entry on Step 4 replicas. Triage tier is now 5 x 10 ns per complex, not 3 x 10 ns. Three remains the absolute floor. Reason is power, not rule compliance: the Step 4 unit of analysis is the design, not the replica, so at the planned 20 vs 20 sets the scalar needs a true AUC of about 0.70 to put its bootstrap CI clear of 0.5 (Hanley-McNeil SE 0.083 at AUC 0.70, 0.078 at 0.75). - 2026-11-09: SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-05 justification for preferring replicas over trajectory length. The real reason is that neither 10 ns nor 100 ns observes unbinding. For a micromolar binder k_off puts the complex lifetime in the millisecond to second range, five to nine orders of magnitude beyond either window, and non-binders do not dissociate inside it either. The Step 4 observable is therefore early relaxation away from the AF2 pose under CHARMM36m, not dissociation. Because the signal is a relaxation basin rather than a rare event, independent replicas sample it better than a longer single trajectory.

- 2026-11-10: BSA convention recorded: bsa = SASA(binder alone) + SASA(target alone) - SASA(complex), the unhalved total, computed by Shrake-Rupley (J Mol Biol 79, 1973, 351-371) with a 1.4 Angstrom water probe, 960 golden-spiral points per atom, and Bondi van der Waals radii (J Phys Chem 68, 1964, 441-451). Hydrogens excluded because AF2 output has none, so these are heavy-atom SASAs; the offset cancels in bsa because it is a difference over identical atom sets. The halved PISA-style value is returned separately as bsa_per_side; nothing is silently halved. An element missing from the radii table raises rather than defaulting to carbon.
