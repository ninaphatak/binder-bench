- 2026-11-09: SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-05 entry on Step 4 replicas. Triage tier is now 5 x 10 ns per complex, not 3 x 10 ns. Three remains the absolute floor. Reason is power, not rule compliance: the Step 4 unit of analysis is the design, not the replica, so at the planned 20 vs 20 sets the scalar needs a true AUC of about 0.70 to put its bootstrap CI clear of 0.5 (Hanley-McNeil SE 0.083 at AUC 0.70, 0.078 at 0.75). Replicas do not change that n; they shrink the measurement error on each design's scalar so the observed AUC approaches the true one. Simulation at a true 1.0 SD separation, 20 vs 20: with within-design noise equal to 1.5x between-design SD the observed AUC is 0.652 at one replica, 0.703 at three, 0.721 at five. One replica lands below the detectability floor. Five x 10 ns costs the same GPU-hours as one 50 ns run.
- 2026-11-09: SUPERSEDES the 2026-11-05 justification for preferring replicas over trajectory length. The real reason is that neither 10 ns nor 100 ns observes unbinding. For a micromolar binder k_off puts the complex lifetime in the millisecond to second range, five to nine orders of magnitude beyond either window, and non-binders do not dissociate inside it either. The Step 4 observable is therefore early relaxation away from the AF2 pose under CHARMM36m, not dissociation. Because the signal is a relaxation basin rather than a rare event, independent replicas sample it better than a longer single trajectory.
